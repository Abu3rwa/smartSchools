GRADEBOOK PRO - PLATFORM GUIDE FOR SCHOOLS
Audience: Teachers, Department Principals, School Owners, Admin Teams
Purpose: Marketing + User Guide (Non-Technical)

WHAT PROBLEM SCHOOLS FACE
Many schools run daily operations using disconnected tools: one system for attendance, one for grades, another for parent communication, and manual spreadsheets for planning. This creates delays, confusion, and communication gaps.

HOW THIS APP SOLVES IT
GradeBook Pro combines academic operations, communication, parent engagement, and school-level leadership controls in one unified platform. Teams work faster, parents stay informed, and school leaders get clearer visibility.

CORE BENEFITS FOR YOUR SCHOOL
1. One connected platform for academic and operational workflows.
2. Clear role-based access so every user sees only what they need.
3. Faster communication with parents, teachers, and students.
4. Better consistency in grading, planning, and student follow-up.
5. Actionable insight for leadership through dashboards and reports.

MAIN ROLES IN THE PLATFORM
- School Owner / Super Admin: Multi-school governance, subscription oversight, and global control.
- School Admin: Full school operations management.
- Department Principal: Department-level leadership and approvals.
- Teacher: Daily teaching, grading, assignments, lesson planning, communication.
- Parent: Child progress visibility, updates, and school communication.
- Student: Learning tasks, revision, practice, and progress tracking.
- Support Staff: Scoped operational support where assigned.
