COMMUNICATION, MESSAGING, EMAIL, NEWSLETTER, AND NOTIFICATIONS MODULE
Covered platform modules: messages, communication email composer, newsletters, notifications, email delivery

SCHOOL PROBLEM
School communication is often inconsistent, delayed, and hard to track across teachers, departments, and families.

APP SOLUTION
The communication stack supports direct messaging, school email campaigns, structured newsletters, and update notifications with role-based scope controls.

HOW SCHOOLS USE THIS MODULE
1. Teachers send classroom-level updates.
2. Department principals send department announcements.
3. Admin teams send school-wide communication and newsletter issues.
4. Parents receive organized updates through preferred channels.

ROLES AND PERMISSIONS FOCUS
Important communication permissions include:
- Message own students and own student parents
- Message department students/parents/teachers/everyone
- Message school students/parents/teachers/everyone
- Send communication emails

GREAT FEATURES TO HIGHLIGHT
- Right message, right audience, right time.
- Less confusion from scattered channels.
- Better communication accountability.
- Improved parent satisfaction and trust.
