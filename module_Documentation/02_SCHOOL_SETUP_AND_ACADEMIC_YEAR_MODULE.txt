SCHOOL SETUP AND ACADEMIC YEAR MODULE
Covered platform modules: schools, school profile, academic year setup, rollover controls, school-level settings

SCHOOL PROBLEM
School-wide settings are often spread across files and people, leading to inconsistent calendars, unclear academic years, and confusion at promotion time.

APP SOLUTION
This module gives school leadership one home for school identity, academic year controls, annual rollover, and key configuration decisions.

HOW SCHOOLS USE THIS MODULE
1. Configure school profile and annual settings.
2. Set current academic year and key date boundaries.
3. Use rollover tools to copy classes and promote students into the new year.
4. Keep year-end operations controlled by leadership.

ROLES AND PERMISSIONS FOCUS
- School Owner / Super Admin: Organization-wide oversight.
- Admin: Full control for school setup and annual operations.
- Department Principal and Teacher: Visibility based on role needs.

GREAT FEATURES TO HIGHLIGHT
- Academic year confidence with structured rollover.
- Clean school profile and branding management.
- Centralized communication and reminder settings.
- Leadership-ready control without technical complexity.
