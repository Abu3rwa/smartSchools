REPORTS, ADVANCED REPORTS, AND CALENDAR MODULE
Covered platform modules: reports, advanced reports, calendar, school calendar

SCHOOL PROBLEM
Leadership decisions are delayed when report data is scattered or not easy to interpret.

APP SOLUTION
This module gives school leaders consolidated reporting and calendar visibility to make timely, informed decisions.

HOW SCHOOLS USE THIS MODULE
1. Generate school and academic performance reports.
2. Use advanced reports for deeper trend analysis.
3. Track events and calendar decisions across the school year.
4. Align departments around one source of truth.

ROLES AND PERMISSIONS FOCUS
- Admin and School Owner: broad report visibility.
- Department Principal: scoped trend oversight.
- Teacher: role-relevant report access where allowed.

GREAT FEATURES TO HIGHLIGHT
- Faster leadership decisions.
- Clearer academic and operational trends.
- Better planning alignment across teams.
- More confidence in school strategy discussions.
