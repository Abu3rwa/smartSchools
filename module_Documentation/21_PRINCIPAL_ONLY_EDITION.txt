PRINCIPAL-ONLY EDITION
Audience: Department Principals and School Principals
Purpose: Leadership playbook for instructional quality, team accountability, and department performance

WHAT PRINCIPALS STRUGGLE WITH
Principals are expected to protect teaching quality, raise student outcomes, manage communication, and maintain consistency across teams. In many schools, this work is slowed by scattered systems and unclear responsibility lines.

HOW GRADEBOOK PRO HELPS PRINCIPALS
This platform gives principals one connected operating view: who is teaching what, what students are learning, where support is needed, and how communication is flowing to families.

YOUR LEADERSHIP DASHBOARD IN PRACTICE
1. Set expectations and monitor execution across planning, attendance, and grading.
2. Review department and class trends without waiting for manual reports.
3. Approve key instructional workflows (curriculum and planning) with clear accountability.
4. Guide interventions early when student or behavior risk appears.
5. Keep communication organized for staff, students, and parents.

PRINCIPAL ROLE AND PERMISSION FOCUS
Common principal permissions include:
- Review lesson plans and curriculum maps.
- Approve, reject, and publish curriculum map workflows.
- View department-level reports and performance trends.
- Manage substitution workflows for continuity.
- Send department-level communications.
- View homework submission trends and instructional follow-through.

WHAT YOU CAN LEAD MODULE BY MODULE

1) Department Governance and Team Accountability
Problem: Different teachers follow different routines, creating inconsistent outcomes.
Solution: Role and permission controls create clear ownership and decision authority.
How principals use it:
- Confirm who can create, review, approve, and publish key academic work.
- Align department members to consistent operating standards.
- Keep delegated access controlled and transparent.

2) Curriculum and Lesson Quality Oversight
Problem: Curriculum quality varies when review cycles are unstructured.
Solution: Structured lesson plan and curriculum workflows with clear review stages.
How principals use it:
- Review teacher submissions.
- Approve or return for revision with expectations.
- Publish curriculum only when quality standards are met.

3) Attendance and School Rhythm Control
Problem: Attendance habits slip without principal-level follow-up.
Solution: Attendance workflows with reminder settings and trend visibility.
How principals use it:
- Monitor attendance consistency by class/teacher patterns.
- Coordinate with admin to strengthen follow-through.
- Escalate repeated issues early.

4) Academic Delivery Consistency (Assignments and Grading)
Problem: Students receive uneven assignment and grading experiences.
Solution: Shared assignment and grading workflows across teams.
How principals use it:
- Review submission and grading completion trends.
- Spot classes that need coaching or support.
- Drive consistency in expectations and feedback quality.

5) Standards and Mastery Monitoring
Problem: Schools often track marks but miss mastery progress.
Solution: Standards-linked practice, reading, revision, and review workflows.
How principals use it:
- Watch where students are progressing or stalling.
- Support teachers with targeted intervention plans.
- Keep department outcomes aligned to mastery goals.

6) Behavior and Intervention Leadership
Problem: Behavior follow-up is often reactive and inconsistent.
Solution: Central behavior records and intervention tracking.
How principals use it:
- Review recurring behavior patterns.
- Coordinate interventions with teaching teams.
- Ensure follow-up actions are completed and documented.

7) Communication Trust Across School and Families
Problem: Mixed communication quality causes confusion and parent dissatisfaction.
Solution: Structured messaging, newsletters, and scoped communication permissions.
How principals use it:
- Lead department-wide communication standards.
- Ensure messages are timely, clear, and audience-correct.
- Reduce noise and raise trust with families.

8) Continuity During Teacher Absence
Problem: Unplanned absences disrupt learning quickly.
Solution: Substitution workflows that speed coverage and confirmation.
How principals use it:
- Review replacement options quickly.
- Confirm assignment and maintain class continuity.
- Track open and resolved coverage requests.

GREAT FEATURES FOR PRINCIPALS TO PROMOTE
- Clear department leadership control without micromanagement.
- Faster instructional decision-making.
- Reliable approval workflows with accountability.
- Better student support through early intervention signals.
- Stronger parent trust through consistent communication.

90-DAY PRINCIPAL SUCCESS PLAYBOOK
Days 1-30
1. Confirm your department permissions and team roles.
2. Audit lesson/curriculum review expectations.
3. Set weekly attendance and homework completion checkpoints.

Days 31-60
1. Standardize communication cadence to families.
2. Track behavior and intervention follow-through.
3. Coach teachers using trend data, not assumptions.

Days 61-90
1. Tighten curriculum approval quality standards.
2. Reduce operational bottlenecks (substitution, reporting delays).
3. Present a department progress snapshot to school leadership.
