STANDARDS, PRACTICE, READING, REVISION, AND REVIEW MODULE
Covered platform modules: standards, standard assignments, practice, reading, revision, review queue

SCHOOL PROBLEM
Schools often lack a structured way to connect standards mastery with daily student practice and revision.

APP SOLUTION
This learning module links standards to assignments, practice sessions, reading support, revision plans, and student review routines.

HOW SCHOOLS USE THIS MODULE
1. Define standards and attach them to instruction.
2. Deliver practice and assessment opportunities.
3. Support student reading progression and comprehension.
4. Use revision and review workflows for mastery growth over time.

ROLES AND PERMISSIONS FOCUS
- Teacher/Admin: monitor progress and release outcomes.
- Student: complete practice, reading activities, revision plans, and review tasks.
- Department Principal: visibility into department learning quality.

GREAT FEATURES TO HIGHLIGHT
- Standards-linked learning clarity.
- Ongoing mastery tracking, not one-time testing.
- Student-centered revision and review habits.
- Better academic recovery for struggling learners.
