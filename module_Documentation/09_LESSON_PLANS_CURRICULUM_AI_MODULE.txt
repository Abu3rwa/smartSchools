LESSON PLANS, CURRICULUM MAPS, AND AI SUPPORT MODULE
Covered platform modules: lesson plans, lesson plan criteria, curriculum maps, curriculum settings, AI-assisted planning

SCHOOL PROBLEM
Teachers and principals lose time creating plans in inconsistent formats, and curriculum approval cycles become slow.

APP SOLUTION
The platform standardizes lesson planning and curriculum mapping, with AI-assisted drafting and structured review/approval paths.

HOW SCHOOLS USE THIS MODULE
1. Teachers draft lesson plans and curriculum maps.
2. Use criteria and templates for consistency.
3. Department principals review and approve where required.
4. Export/publish plans for classroom and leadership use.

ROLES AND PERMISSIONS FOCUS
Important permissions include:
- Edit and Review Lesson Plans
- Create/Edit/Review/Approve/Publish Curriculum Maps
- Configure Curriculum Templates

GREAT FEATURES TO HIGHLIGHT
- Less planning time, more teaching time.
- Consistent instructional quality.
- Clear approval accountability for principals.
- AI support for faster high-quality draft content.
