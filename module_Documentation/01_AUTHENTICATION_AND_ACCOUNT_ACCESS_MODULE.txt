AUTHENTICATION AND ACCOUNT ACCESS MODULE
Covered platform modules: auth, user account access, login/session management

SCHOOL PROBLEM
When account access is hard to manage, teachers lose time, parents cannot log in, and support requests increase.

APP SOLUTION
The platform provides structured login and account access workflows so every role can securely access their own workspace and continue working without confusion.

HOW SCHOOLS USE THIS MODULE
1. Admin creates and activates staff/student/parent accounts.
2. Users sign in to role-based dashboards.
3. Admin supports password reset or account status updates when needed.
4. School teams keep access clean during staff changes and student year transitions.

ROLES AND PERMISSIONS FOCUS
- Admin and School Owner: Full account lifecycle management.
- Department Principal: Scoped account support where delegated.
- Teacher/Student/Parent: Access only their own role-specific workspace.

GREAT FEATURES TO HIGHLIGHT
- Reliable login experience across roles.
- Clear account ownership per school.
- Faster onboarding for new users.
- Better continuity during term changes and staffing updates.
