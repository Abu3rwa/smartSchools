PUBLIC SITE, LANDING, AND ENROLLMENT JOURNEY MODULE
Covered platform modules: public routes, landing content, school registration/pricing presentation

SCHOOL PROBLEM
Schools need a professional first impression and a clean enrollment/interest journey for families and stakeholders.

APP SOLUTION
The public module supports external-facing school presentation, structured landing pages, and guided registration pathways.

HOW SCHOOLS USE THIS MODULE
1. Present school value and offerings clearly through landing content.
2. Share pricing and onboarding information where relevant.
3. Capture school registration interest and route it for onboarding.
4. Keep the public journey aligned with school brand.

ROLES AND PERMISSIONS FOCUS
- School Owner/Admin: manage public-facing positioning and onboarding readiness.
- Families and visitors: consume clear, trustworthy school information.

GREAT FEATURES TO HIGHLIGHT
- Strong first impression for prospective families.
- Consistent message from marketing to onboarding.
- Better conversion from interest to registration.
- Professional digital presence for school growth.
