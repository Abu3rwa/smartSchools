BEHAVIOR, STUDENT BEHAVIOR, AND INTERVENTION MODULE
Covered platform modules: behavior analytics, student behavior records, interventions

SCHOOL PROBLEM
Behavior incidents are often logged in scattered places, making follow-up inconsistent and late.

APP SOLUTION
This module centralizes behavior events, student follow-up records, and intervention tracking so teams can respond early and consistently.

HOW SCHOOLS USE THIS MODULE
1. Record incidents and behavior patterns in a shared system.
2. Review class and student summaries.
3. Assign intervention actions and monitor status.
4. Use leadership dashboards for prevention-focused decision making.

ROLES AND PERMISSIONS FOCUS
- Admin / Super Admin: behavior dashboards and analytics.
- Department Principal: department-level behavior oversight.
- Teacher: submit and track relevant behavior records.
- Assigned staff: support interventions under scope.

GREAT FEATURES TO HIGHLIGHT
- Earlier intervention before issues escalate.
- Better consistency in discipline workflows.
- Data-informed behavior support.
- Improved student wellbeing outcomes.
