SUBSTITUTIONS AND DAILY OPERATIONS CONTINUITY MODULE
Covered platform modules: substitutions, teacher absence support, replacement workflow coordination

SCHOOL PROBLEM
When substitute workflows are manual, classes lose instructional continuity and leaders face urgent daily disruption.

APP SOLUTION
Substitution workflows help schools manage teacher absence coverage with better speed, visibility, and confirmation.

HOW SCHOOLS USE THIS MODULE
1. Record teacher absence and required cover.
2. Review available substitute options.
3. Confirm assignment and notify relevant staff.
4. Track active requests and close completed coverage.

ROLES AND PERMISSIONS FOCUS
- Department Principal and Admin: substitution management control.
- Teacher: can interact with assigned substitution actions where allowed.

GREAT FEATURES TO HIGHLIGHT
- Reduced disruption from unexpected absences.
- Faster substitution turnaround.
- Better communication during schedule changes.
- More stable classroom learning continuity.
