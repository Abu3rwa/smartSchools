USER ROLES, PERMISSIONS, AND DEPARTMENTS MODULE
Covered platform modules: user management, permissions, departments, scoped access policies

SCHOOL PROBLEM
Without clear permissions, either too many people can edit sensitive data or too few can complete daily tasks quickly.

APP SOLUTION
Role and permission controls allow schools to assign exactly who can view, edit, approve, publish, message, and manage each part of operations.

HOW SCHOOLS USE THIS MODULE
1. Define who is Admin, Department Principal, Teacher, Parent, Student, or Staff.
2. Assign department ownership for principals and relevant staff.
3. Grant specific permissions for critical tasks (grading, homework publishing, communication, curriculum approvals).
4. Review user access regularly to keep governance clean.

ROLES AND PERMISSIONS FOCUS
Important permission groups include:
- Academic permissions: grading, assignments, homework, curriculum workflows.
- Communication permissions: who can message own classes, department groups, or whole school.
- Operational permissions: attendance reminders, behavior records, reports, and settings.

GREAT FEATURES TO HIGHLIGHT
- Role-based trust and accountability.
- Department-scoped leadership workflows.
- Better protection of student and school information.
- Flexibility to match each school's leadership model.
