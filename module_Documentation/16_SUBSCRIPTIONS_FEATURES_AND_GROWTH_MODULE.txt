SUBSCRIPTIONS, FEATURE ACCESS, AND SCHOOL GROWTH MODULE
Covered platform modules: subscriptions, plan features, usage limits, pricing visibility

SCHOOL PROBLEM
Schools need clear visibility into what they can use today and what additional capabilities unlock as they grow.

APP SOLUTION
Subscription and feature controls align school plan level with enabled capabilities such as parent portal, advanced analytics, custom reporting, AI email drafts, and branding options.

HOW SCHOOLS USE THIS MODULE
1. School owner/admin reviews current plan and enabled features.
2. Leadership aligns feature needs with growth stage.
3. Manage billing and plan updates as the school expands.
4. Communicate feature availability clearly to teams.

ROLES AND PERMISSIONS FOCUS
- School Owner / Super Admin: plan and portfolio oversight.
- Admin: school-level feature operations.
- Teachers and principals: use enabled capabilities within role scope.

GREAT FEATURES TO HIGHLIGHT
- Plan clarity tied to real school outcomes.
- Scalable path from starter to enterprise readiness.
- Better budgeting visibility.
- Controlled rollout of advanced capabilities.
