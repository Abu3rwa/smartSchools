TEACHER-ONLY EDITION
Audience: Classroom Teachers
Purpose: Daily teaching workflow guide for planning, instruction, grading, communication, and student support

WHAT TEACHERS STRUGGLE WITH
Teachers often work across many tools for planning, attendance, assignments, parent updates, and follow-up. This creates duplicate work and takes time away from teaching.

HOW GRADEBOOK PRO HELPS TEACHERS
The platform brings your daily work into one connected flow so you can plan faster, teach better, grade consistently, and communicate clearly with families.

YOUR DAILY TEACHING FLOW
1. Check timetable and class schedule.
2. Record attendance.
3. Deliver assignments/homework and track submissions.
4. Grade and review student progress trends.
5. Send classroom communication to students and parents.
6. Update lesson plans and complete required submissions.

TEACHER ROLE AND PERMISSION FOCUS
Common teacher permissions include:
- Edit lesson plans.
- Create, publish, and grade assignments.
- Create, publish, and grade homework.
- View grades and update grade records.
- Access classroom-level communication scopes (own students and own student parents).
- Use classroom learning modules for standards, practice, reading, revision, and review.

WHAT YOU CAN DO MODULE BY MODULE

1) Class and Timetable Clarity
Problem: Daily confusion wastes teaching time.
Solution: One place for your timetable, periods, and classroom schedule.
How teachers use it:
- Review your classes and teaching times before the school day.
- Respond quickly to schedule updates.
- Keep classroom routines stable.

2) Attendance with Less Friction
Problem: Attendance takes time and may be delayed during busy days.
Solution: Streamlined attendance workflow with support from school reminders.
How teachers use it:
- Mark attendance quickly during/after class.
- Keep records accurate for student and parent trust.
- Help leadership catch attendance concerns early.

3) Assignments and Homework That Stay Organized
Problem: Assignment management becomes heavy across multiple classes.
Solution: Unified assignment and homework lifecycle in one system.
How teachers use it:
- Create and publish work for your classes.
- Track submissions and completion rates.
- Keep work distribution and deadlines clear.

4) Grading That Is Consistent and Transparent
Problem: Grading across classes can become inconsistent under pressure.
Solution: Connected grading workflows with clear scale structure.
How teachers use it:
- Grade in the same system where work is assigned.
- Keep grading records current.
- Help students and parents see progress clearly.

5) Better Lesson Planning, Faster
Problem: Planning quality is hard to maintain when time is limited.
Solution: Lesson planning with structured criteria and AI-assisted drafting support.
How teachers use it:
- Build lesson plans quickly with consistent structure.
- Improve draft quality before submission.
- Align plans to school and department expectations.

6) Standards and Student Mastery Support
Problem: Students need targeted practice, not generic repetition.
Solution: Standards-linked practice, reading, revision, and review tools.
How teachers use it:
- Assign standards-focused work.
- Monitor mastery and identify gaps.
- Give students targeted next steps.

7) Classroom Communication That Families Understand
Problem: Parent communication can be scattered and repetitive.
Solution: Messaging and communication tools with audience control.
How teachers use it:
- Send class updates to your students and their parents.
- Keep messages simple, timely, and consistent.
- Reduce confusion and repeated follow-up questions.

8) Student Behavior and Support Escalation
Problem: Behavior concerns are hard to track if records are fragmented.
Solution: Structured behavior and intervention support workflows.
How teachers use it:
- Log relevant incidents.
- Track follow-up and support actions.
- Collaborate with principals and support teams early.
